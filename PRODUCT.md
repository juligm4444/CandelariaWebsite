# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- **Companies and institutions evaluating a sponsorship.** They arrive from a
  pitch or a referral, read on desktop, and need to judge in a few minutes
  whether the group is serious: what it builds, how it decides, where money
  goes, who to talk to.
- **Universidad de los Andes** (faculty, communications, other students). They
  check that the group is active, rigorous and well run.
- **Private supporters.** People who want to back the project with a recurring
  membership. They get an account to follow progress and manage billing, but a
  membership never makes them part of the organisation.
- **Members of the group.** They publish to the log and manage their area from
  the internal dashboard, and keep their own public team card up to date.

Recruitment into the group happens through a separate channel and process, not
through this site.

## Product Purpose

Present Candelaria Solar Car, Universidad de los Andes' interdisciplinary
research group that designs, builds and validates a competition solar vehicle,
as a credible engineering project: the vehicle and its subsystems, the seven
areas and the people in them, a technical log written by the areas, and a clear
way to support or sponsor it. Success is a sponsor who leaves knowing what the
group does and how to reach the Committee, and a supporter who completes a
membership without friction.

## Brand Personality

Technical, ambitious, rigorous. A technical team that knows how to communicate:
clear, confident and close. Ambitious without arrogance, technical without
being hermetic, collaborative without being informal, optimistic without being
naive. The fact before the adjective.

## Anti-references

- Hype and superlatives ("¡¡somos los mejores!!"), double exclamation marks,
  emoji in official copy.
- Generic SaaS landing pages: hero-metric templates, identical icon-card grids,
  gradient text.
- Figures without a source. Invented telemetry, invented percentages or
  invented compliance results presented as real. Anything provisional is
  labelled as provisional on the page.
- Generated photographs of the vehicle or of people presented as the real car
  or the real team.

## Design Principles

1. **The fact before the adjective.** Every claim is backed by a model, a
   measurement or a regulation, or it is visibly marked as pending.
2. **Show the engineering.** Structure the site around the vehicle, the
   subsystems and the areas that own them; let data tables, matrices and specs
   carry the page.
3. **The brand manual is binding.** `docs/brand/candelaria-marca.md` overrides
   generic design defaults: one deep-purple theme, Sansation and Helvetica Now,
   sizes in multiples of 8, no shadows.
4. **Honest states.** Empty, provisional and pending states are designed, not
   hidden.
5. **Two audiences, two journeys.** Private supporters and companies get
   separate, explicit paths.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA minimum: 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text, with area colours
split into an `accent` token (bars and borders) and a contrast-checked `label`
token (text). Every animation respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Content is
visible by default and never gated on a JavaScript reveal. The site is bilingual
(Spanish and English) and works with JavaScript disabled wherever a filter or
form allows it.
